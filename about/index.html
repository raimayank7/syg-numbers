Numbers

About Numbers

Numbers is a free site about how numbers are written and read: number names in English in the Indian system (lakh, crore) and the international system (million, billion), in Hindi, with place values, commas, expanded form and Roman numerals. It has a converter, charts to read and print, and plain guides.

Where the content comes from

  • The number names are produced by our own program and checked in several ways when the site is built: the English words are compared with the open-source num2words package and are also read back into figures by a separate routine; the Hindi words are read back the same way; Roman numerals are made by two different methods that must agree. The converter in the page is tested against the same results.
  • All explanations are our own original writing. The rules described are standard school mathematics and everyday usage in India.
  • The Hindi spellings follow the forms common in current school books (छह, पाँच, हज़ार). Other accepted spellings exist. The Hindi has not yet been read by a second native reader. If a word reads oddly to you, please tell us.
  • The page on writing a cheque amount describes common practice and is not banking advice. Ask your bank about its own requirements.

For printable practice, see number names, place value and Roman numerals on our Worksheets site.
