Why two systems?

India names large numbers in lakhs and crores, and writes commas after three digits and then every two. Most other countries use millions and billions, with commas every three digits. A reader in India meets both every day: a salary in lakhs, a company result in millions of dollars. The number itself does not change, only how it is cut into groups and named. This site shows both, side by side, so that either can be read with ease.

Common questions

How do I write 1,50,000 in words?

One lakh fifty thousand in the Indian system, and one hundred fifty thousand in the international system. In Hindi it is एक लाख पचास हज़ार.

What is the difference between the Indian and the international system?

The digits are the same. The Indian system groups them as 3, 2, 2 and uses lakh and crore. The international system groups them in threes and uses million and billion. One million is ten lakh, and one crore is ten million.

Is it forty or fourty?

Forty. Four and fourteen have a "u", but forty does not.

Does the converter store my numbers?

No. It works inside your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
